Đoạn đọc kiểu Part 7

Travel Expense Policy (updated January 1)

Employees traveling on company business may claim reimbursement for transportation, accommodation and meals. Hotel rates must not exceed $150 per night. Meal expenses are limited to $50 per day. All claims must be submitted within 14 days of returning, together with original receipts. Claims submitted after this period will require approval from a department director.

1. What is the maximum amount an employee can claim for a hotel room each night? (A) $50 (B) $100 (C) $150 (D) $200

2. What must be included with a claim? (A) A boarding pass (B) A director's signature (C) Original receipts (D) A travel itinerary

3. What happens if a claim is submitted on the 20th day after returning? (A) It will be rejected automatically. (B) It needs a director's approval. (C) It will be paid in installments. (D) The employee must pay a fee.

Xem đáp án

1. C. 2. C. 3. B – quá 14 ngày cần approval from a department director. Đáp án A là bẫy suy diễn quá mức: bài không nói bị từ chối.

Bài tập

Chọn từ đúng: (1) The taxi (fare / fee) to the airport is about $30. (2) The registration (fare / fee) includes lunch. (3) The invoice is now (overdue / overtime). (4) Please (reimburse / refund) my travel costs. (5) The (venue / avenue) for the conference has changed.

Xem đáp án

(1) fare (2) fee (3) overdue (4) reimburse – chi phí mình ứng trước cho công ty; refund là hoàn tiền mua hàng (5) venue.
