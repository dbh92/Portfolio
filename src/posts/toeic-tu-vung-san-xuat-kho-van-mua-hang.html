Nếu bạn làm ở nhà máy, kho vận hay phòng mua hàng, đây là chủ đề vừa giúp thi TOEIC vừa dùng được ngay hôm sau. Part 3–4 hay có hội thoại về giao hàng trễ, đơn hàng sai, máy hỏng. Part 7 hay có đơn đặt hàng, hóa đơn, thông báo bảo trì. Bài này gom khoảng 60 từ theo bốn nhóm, kèm mẫu câu báo cáo sự cố trên dây chuyền.

Đoạn đọc kiểu Part 7

Dear Mr. Hoang,

Thank you for your order of 2,000 plastic housings (PO #5521). Unfortunately, one of our molding machines malfunctioned last week, so we can only ship 1,200 units on May 10 as scheduled. The remaining 800 units will be dispatched by May 17. To apologize for the inconvenience, we will cover the freight charges for the second shipment. Please let me know if this arrangement is acceptable.

Best regards,
Kim Su-jin, Sales Department, Daehan Plastics

1. Why is part of the order delayed? (A) Raw materials are out of stock. (B) Some equipment stopped working. (C) The order was placed late. (D) The shipping company is on strike.

2. What does Ms. Kim offer? (A) A discount on the next order (B) Free shipping for the remaining units (C) A full refund (D) Faster delivery by air

3. How many units will be shipped later than originally planned? (A) 800 (B) 1,200 (C) 2,000 (D) 5,521

Xem đáp án

1. B – molding machines malfunctioned → equipment stopped working. 2. B – cover the freight charges for the second shipment. 3. A – 800 units dispatched by May 17. Đáp án D là bẫy: 5521 là số PO.
