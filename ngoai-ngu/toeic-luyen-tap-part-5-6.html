Part 5: 30 câu, 10 phút

1. The finance team will ______ review all expense reports before the audit. (A) careful (B) carefully (C) care (D) cared

2. Ms. Sato ______ the Tokyo office since it opened in 2018. (A) manages (B) managed (C) has managed (D) is managing

3. ______ the high demand, the store has extended its opening hours. (A) Because (B) Due to (C) Although (D) So that

4. All participants will receive a certificate upon ______ of the course. (A) complete (B) completed (C) completion (D) completely

5. The keynote speech, ______ was given by a famous economist, attracted 500 people. (A) who (B) that (C) which (D) whose

6. If you have any questions about your order, please contact ______ customer service team. (A) we (B) us (C) our (D) ours

7. The prices of raw materials ______ significantly over the past year. (A) rose (B) have risen (C) have been risen (D) are rising

8. The new safety rules must ______ by all factory workers. (A) follow (B) be followed (C) following (D) have followed

9. The hotel is ______ located near the convention center. (A) convenient (B) conveniently (C) convenience (D) convenes

10. Mr. Ahn is the most ______ candidate for the engineering position. (A) qualify (B) qualified (C) qualification (D) qualifying

11. Employees are encouraged ______ ideas for improving efficiency. (A) submit (B) submitting (C) to submit (D) submitted

12. The meeting was ______ until next week because the director was ill. (A) canceled (B) postponed (C) attended (D) arranged

13. The package should arrive ______ three business days. (A) within (B) during (C) since (D) until

14. Neither the printer nor the scanners ______ working properly. (A) is (B) was (C) are (D) has been

15. We look forward to ______ with you on this project. (A) work (B) working (C) worked (D) be working

16. The new model is ______ lighter than the previous one. (A) very (B) much (C) most (D) more

17. ______ you need further assistance, please call the help desk. (A) Should (B) Unless (C) Whether (D) Had

18. The board requested that the report ______ by Friday. (A) is completed (B) be completed (C) completes (D) completing

19. The company offers a ______ salary and excellent benefits. (A) compete (B) competition (C) competitive (D) competitively

20. The software update is expected to ______ the system's security. (A) enhance (B) enhanced (C) enhancement (D) enhancing

21. All visitors must sign in at the front desk ______ entering the building. (A) before (B) until (C) while (D) during

22. The marketing campaign ______ our expectations. (A) exceeded (B) excelled (C) exchanged (D) expired

23. Ms. Lin prepared the presentation by ______. (A) she (B) her (C) hers (D) herself

24. The number of applicants for the internship ______ doubled this year. (A) have (B) has (C) are (D) were

25. The warehouse, ______ is located near the port, will be expanded. (A) where (B) which (C) what (D) that

26. Please send the signed contract to the legal department as ______ as possible. (A) soon (B) sooner (C) soonest (D) early

27. The technician ______ the air conditioner when the power went out. (A) repairs (B) was repairing (C) has repaired (D) will repair

28. Customers ______ purchases exceed $200 will receive a free gift. (A) who (B) whose (C) which (D) whom

29. The factory tour is ______ to employees and their families. (A) open (B) opened (C) opening (D) openly

30. ______ the shipment arrives, we will inspect every box. (A) As soon as (B) In addition to (C) Because of (D) Prior to

Part 6: 2 đoạn, 4 phút

Notice to all tenants

The elevators in Building A will be out of service on Saturday, May 10, from 8 A.M. to 4 P.M. ___(31)___ this time, technicians will replace the main control system. ___(32)___. We recommend that tenants who need to move heavy items ___(33)___ so before Friday evening. We apologize for any ___(34)___ this may cause.

31. (A) While (B) During (C) Since (D) When

32. (A) The stairs will remain open throughout the day. (B) The building was designed by a local architect. (C) Rent is due on the first of each month. (D) Our technicians have ten years of experience.

33. (A) do (B) does (C) doing (D) to do

34. (A) inconvenient (B) inconveniently (C) inconvenience (D) inconvenienced

Dear Mr. Bui,

Thank you for applying for the position of logistics coordinator at Pacific Freight. We were impressed by your experience in warehouse management. ___(35)___, we would like to invite you to an interview on Thursday, June 5, at 10 A.M. The interview will ___(36)___ about one hour. ___(37)___. Please confirm your attendance by replying to this e-mail no ___(38)___ than Monday.

35. (A) However (B) Therefore (C) Otherwise (D) Instead

36. (A) last (B) spend (C) take place (D) continue

37. (A) Please bring a copy of your résumé and a photo ID. (B) Pacific Freight was founded in 1998. (C) The position has been filled. (D) We ship goods to 30 countries.

38. (A) late (B) later (C) latest (D) lately

Đếm số câu sai theo cột "Loại". Hai loại sai nhiều nhất là việc của tuần tới. Nếu bạn đúng nhiều nhưng vượt 14 phút, hãy luyện thêm bước phân loại nhanh ở bài chiến thuật Part 5.

Bài tập thực hành

  1. Làm toàn bộ bài luyện trong 14 phút, chấm điểm theo bảng phân loại trong bài.
  2. Chọn nhóm câu sai nhiều nhất, xem lại bài lý thuyết tương ứng và làm lại các câu sai sau 3 ngày.

Làm xong bài tập mới chuyển sang bài tiếp theo: tự làm, nói thành tiếng và chữa lỗi là cách duy nhất để nhớ lâu.
